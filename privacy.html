Privacy Policy

UPCODEZ OTT Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UPCODEZ OTT ("we", "our", "the App") handles information when you use our Android application and website. We designed UPCODEZ OTT with a simple, minimal approach — we do not require an account, and we do not collect personal information beyond what is strictly necessary for the App to function.

1 Information We Collect

UPCODEZ OTT does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, phone number or location to use the App.

The App may process the following limited information to operate correctly:

  • Search queries you enter within the App, used only in real time to return matching results on your device.
  • Basic technical data such as app version and device type, used for compatibility and stability purposes.
  • Anonymous usage signals (if enabled) to help us understand general app stability. These do not identify you personally.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, precise location, or any sensitive personal data.

2 How We Use Information

Any information processed by UPCODEZ OTT is used only for the following purposes:

  • To display video content and search results correctly.
  • To maintain and improve app stability and performance.
  • To fix bugs and ensure the App continues to work properly on your device.
  • To keep the App safe and prevent misuse.

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3 Third-Party Content and Services

UPCODEZ OTT displays video content through third-party hosting and embedding services. When you play a video, the respective third-party service may process data according to its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these providers.

Examples of third-party services that may be involved include video hosting and embedding platforms. UPCODEZ OTT does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

4 Data Storage

UPCODEZ OTT does not store your personal data on external servers. Any preferences or app settings remain on your device. When you uninstall the App, this local data is removed.

5 Permissions

UPCODEZ OTT only requests the minimum permissions required for the App to work, such as internet access to load video content. We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or location unless a future feature explicitly requires it — in which case we will clearly inform you and ask for your permission.

6 Children's Privacy

UPCODEZ OTT is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7 Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the App and any information processed through it. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Your Choices

You can stop using UPCODEZ OTT at any time by uninstalling the App from your device. Since we do not maintain user accounts, there is no account data to delete.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10 Contact
